How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

Our mortgage calculator helps you estimate your monthly house payment including principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and private mortgage insurance (PMI). Enter your home price, down payment, loan term, and interest rate to see your complete payment breakdown.

Understanding Your Mortgage Payment

Principal & Interest

The core of your mortgage payment. Principal pays down your loan balance, while interest is the cost of borrowing.

Escrow (Taxes & Insurance)

Most lenders require you to pay property taxes and insurance monthly as part of your mortgage payment.

15-Year vs 30-Year Mortgage

A 15-year mortgage typically has a lower interest rate but higher monthly payments. You'll pay significantly less interest over the life of the loan. A 30-year mortgage offers lower monthly payments but costs more in interest over time. Use our calculator to compare both options side by side.
